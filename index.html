What is the device layer?

Every digital decision bottoms out somewhere physical: a chip that holds a key, a screen that shows you what you're approving, a button under your thumb. The device layer is that bottom — the boundary where human intent becomes cryptographic authorization, and where software's promises finally have to be kept by hardware.

This publication studies trust at that boundary: between people and interfaces, software and silicon, self-custody and institutions, human authority and autonomous agents. The recurring argument across the editions is that trust can't be removed from these systems — only made auditable. The site tries to practice what the essays preach: every substantive piece carries a Trust Ledger of claims checked, sources, commercial interests, and open uncertainties.
