Privacy

Edition 5 argues that every piece of data has to justify its own existence. This site holds itself to that.

No analytics, no trackers, no ads
This site runs no analytics scripts, no tracking pixels, no advertising, and no social-media embeds. Share what you read by copying the URL.
No accounts, no cookies
There is nothing to log into. The only thing stored in your browser is your light/dark theme choice (in localStorage, never transmitted).
Fonts
All typefaces are self-hosted on this site's own domain. No font service — or any other third party — receives a request when you load a page.
Hosting
The site is static and served by its host (currently GitHub Pages), which processes IP addresses to deliver pages, as any web host does. The publication itself receives no visitor data.
Email subscription
If you subscribe by email once delivery is live, your address is used to send editions and nothing else — no resale, no enrichment, no "partners." Unsubscribing deletes it.
RSS
The feed is the most private way to follow: your reader fetches it directly and this site learns nothing about you.
